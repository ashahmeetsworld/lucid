Finding your way

04 · PLAY
A tower you always see
One glowing landmark over the city. It points the way and shows how far you've come. No arrows needed.
A map you fill in
New places start blank. The line map draws itself as you walk it, so it becomes a record of you.
Two-second retry
Fights can be hard. Falling costs seconds, not minutes, so you're back in while the lesson is fresh.
Leave and come back
Stuck on a boss? Go explore. Grow the party, then return. The world is the difficulty setting.

Three dials of story

05 · STORY
Plot
What happens
The storyline, stripped bare. Leads only when it's new, has a twist, or has something to say.
Character
Who they are
Hopes, flaws, the small talk by the fire. Told through looks, moves and lines, as much as words.
Lore
The world itself
Every ruin has a reason. Signs, shop names, item notes. The city existed before you arrived.
Draft: a party game wins on character, with lore close behind. Pick the dials first, then write.
